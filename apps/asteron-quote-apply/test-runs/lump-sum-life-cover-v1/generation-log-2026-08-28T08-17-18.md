**Parsing judgment calls (logged):**
- AC23 in the source is TWO rows (the numbered AC23 "enter SI + max 3 Life covers", and an
  unnumbered follow-on "when 3 selected, Life button disabled"). They describe one requirement
  (a 3-cover maximum with the button disabling at the cap). Folded into a single internal ID
  LSC-LIFE-AC-023 with both clauses quoted verbatim in the annotation. Flagged: the button-disable
  behaviour partly conflicts with existing reverse-engineered `LSC-39` ("button remains enabled
  after 2 instances; 3rd needs 2nd SI filled") — the STORY says disabled at 3, so we assert to
  the story and this may be a confirmed-failing / genuine-defect check.
- AC08/AC09 say "age" not "age next birthday" while AC10-14 say "age next birthday". Treated all
  as Age Next Birthday (the only age field the quote screen exposes — `PD-04`). Flagged as a
  minor wording inconsistency in the source, not a behavioural difference.
- AC13 (Level to 80) and AC12 (Level to 75) BOTH state the max is 70. AC13's own message text says
  "level to 80 ... is 70". Kept verbatim per the story even though 80>75 makes the "70" cap look
  copy-paste-suspicious. Flagged for author clarification.
- Error message quotes in the story have unbalanced quotation marks (e.g. AC08 `"...level to 50 "Life Cover" is 45`
  with no closing quote). Normalised the *asserted substring* to the stable, unambiguous core
  (e.g. `level to 50` + `Life Cover` + `is 45`) rather than the malformed full string — logged as
  a matching decision, not a value change.
