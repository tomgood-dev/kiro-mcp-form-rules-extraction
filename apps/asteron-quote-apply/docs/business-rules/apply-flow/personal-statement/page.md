# Personal Statement

> Child of [Apply Flow](../page.md). Rule ID prefix: `PS-`. See the [confidence note](../page.md) on this section.

A 7-page medical and lifestyle questionnaire. All questions are **Yes/No radio buttons** unless otherwise noted. Answering **Yes** to most questions reveals follow-up sub-questions or free-text detail fields (not exhaustively catalogued below — the questions themselves are the primary reference content here).

| Rule ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| `PS-01` | **Completion gate:** all 7 pages must be fully answered before the Underwriting Decision step is reachable. The app tracks unanswered questions and surfaces an **"Unanswered Questions"** summary page before allowing progression, with an Answer button per item linking straight to the specific page/question. |
