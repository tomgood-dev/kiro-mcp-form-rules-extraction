| Story card | Apply for Personal Lump Sum Trauma Cover (Standalone) |
| --- | --- |
| JIRA |   |
| User Story | As an Advisor/Advisor StaffI want to be able to apply for lump sum cover while I am creating a quote for a client So that I am able to add various lumpsum covers to the clients policy. |
| Pre-Conditions |  |
| PC01 | Advisor has been on-boarded (training completed, credentials provided to advisor) |
| PC02 |  |
| Acceptance Criteria |  |
|  AC01 | **Given** I am anAdviser or an Adviser staff **When** I am “creating a new quote” **Then** I should be provided the ability to apply for ‘lump sum cover’ in the quote |
|  AC02 | **Given **I am in the Lump Sum Cover section**When **I am creating a new business quote/application**Then **I am able to see below lump sum covers - Life - TPD - Trauma - Cancer - Acd. Death - Needlestick - Specific Injury  **And **I must be able to select 1 or more covers |
|  AC03 | **Given** I am in the **Lump Sum Cover** section of the **New Business Quoting Tool**, **When** I select **Trauma Coverage**, **Then** I must be able to: 1. **Enter** the **Sum Insured** amount. 2. **Select** the **Premium Structure** from a dropdown with the following options:     - Stepped *(default)*     - Level to 65     - Level to 70 3. **Choose** from the following **optional benefits**:     - Early Trauma Benefit     - Trauma Reinstatement     - Continuous Trauma Benefit 4. **View** the following **additional covers** under **Lump Sum Cover Trauma**:     - Major Trauma     - TPD on Trauma |
| AC04 | **Given** I have selected **Trauma Cover**, **When** I select the **Major Trauma** additional cover, **Then** I must be able to: 1. **Enter** the **Sum Insured** amount for **Major Trauma**. 2. **View** the **Premium Structure** pre-populated and should same as **Trauma Cover**. |
|  AC05 | **Given** I have selected **Trauma Cover**, **When** I select the **TPD on Trauma** additional cover, **Then** I must be able to see: 1. **Sum Insured **which is same as Trauma 2. **Premium Structure** pre-populated and should same as Trauma. 3. **Select** the **Definition** from a dropdown with the following options     1. Own(default)     2. Any |
| AC06 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma Cover**,**When **age next birthday is less than 17**Then **an error message “The minimum Age Next Birthday for Trauma Recovery Cover is 17” should be displayed |
| AC07 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma Cover**,**When **age next birthday is more than 70**And **Premium Structure is Stepped**Then **an error message “The maximum Age Next Birthday for Stepped Trauma Recovery Cover is 70” should be displayed |
| AC08 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma Cover**,**When **age next birthday is more than 60**And **Premium Structure is Level to 65**Then **an error message “The maximum age next birthday for Level to 65 Trauma Recovery cover is 60” should be displayed |
| AC09 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma Cover**,**When **age next birthday is more than 65**And **Premium Structure is Level to 70**Then **an error message “The maximum age next birthday for Level to 70 Trauma Recovery cover is 65” should be displayed |
| AC10 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma Cover**,**When **age next birthday is between 17 to 21**And **Sum Insured is more than 250000**Then **an error message “The maximum total Sum Insured per life for Trauma Recovery Cover, including Cancer Cover, for clients Age Next Birthday 17 - 21 is $250,000.” should be displayed |
| AC11 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma & Cancer Cover**,**When **age next birthday is between 17 to 21**And **combined Sum Insured is more than 250000**Then **an error message “The maximum total Sum Insured per life for Trauma Recovery Cover, including Cancer Cover, for clients Age Next Birthday 17 - 21 is $250,000.” should be displayed |
| AC12 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma & Major Trauma**,**When **age next birthday is between 17 to 21**And **combined Sum Insured is more than 250000**Then **an error message “The maximum total Sum Insured per life for Trauma Recovery Cover, including Cancer Cover, for clients Age Next Birthday 17 - 21 is $250,000.” should be displayed |
| AC13 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma, Cancer & Major Trauma**,**When **age next birthday is between 17 to 21**And **combined Sum Insured is more than 250000**Then **an error message “The maximum total Sum Insured per life for Trauma Recovery Cover, Major Trauma, including Cancer Cover, for clients Age Next Birthday 17 - 21 is $250,000.” should be displayed |
| AC14 | **Given** I have selected **Trauma Cover**, **When** the **Age Next Birthday** is between **22 and 70** (inclusive), **And** the combined **Sum Insured(both business and personal Trauma SI)** exceeds **$2,000,000**, **Then** an error message must be displayed:  **“The maximum total Sum Insured per life for Trauma Recovery Cover, including Cancer Cover, is $2,000,000.”** |
| AC15 | **Given** I have selected **Trauma & Cancer Cover**, **When** the **Age Next Birthday** is between **22 and 70** (inclusive), **And** the **combined Sum Insured** exceeds **$2,000,000**, **Then** an error message must be displayed:  **“The maximum total Sum Insured per life for Trauma Recovery Cover, including Cancer Cover, is $2,000,000.”** |
| AC16 | **Given** I have selected **Trauma & Major Trauma**, **When** the **Age Next Birthday** is between **22 and 70** (inclusive), **And** the **combined Sum Insured** exceeds **$2,000,000**, **Then** an error message must be displayed:  **“The maximum total Sum Insured per life for Trauma Recovery Cover, including Cancer Cover, is $2,000,000.”** |
| AC17 | **Given** I have selected **Trauma, Cancer & Major Trauma**, **When** the **Age Next Birthday** is between **22 and 70** (inclusive), **And** the **combined Sum Insured** exceeds **$2,000,000**, **Then** an error message must be displayed:  **“The maximum total Sum Insured per life for Trauma Recovery Cover, Major Trauma, including Cancer Cover, is $2,000,000.”** |
| AC18 | **Given** I have selected **multiple Trauma covers**, **When** I select up to a **maximum of 3 Trauma covers**, **Then** the **“+Trauma” button** must be: - **Greyed out**, indicating no further additions are allowed. - **Disabled**, preventing the user from adding any more Trauma covers. |
|  AC19 | **When **I have selected the cover type**Then **I must be provided the option to ‘add’ or ‘remove’ the cover type or update details**And **I must be able to view the changes to the premiums on the “Progress Panel for application progress” |
| AC20 | **Given** **AC03** is active**When** I select **one** of the following options: - **Trauma Reinstatement** - **Continuous Trauma Benefit** **Then**: - The **other option** must be **greyed out** and **disabled** - I must **not** be able to select both options simultaneously |
| AC21 | **Given** I have selected **Trauma Cover**, **When** the **Age Next Birthday** is between **22 and 70** (inclusive), **And** the **Sum Insured** less than **$5,000**, **Then** an error message must be displayed:  **“The minimum Trauma Cover sum insured is $5,000.”** |
| AC22 | **Given** I have selected **Trauma Cover and Major Trauma**, **When** the **Age Next Birthday** is between **22 and 70** (inclusive), **And** the **Sum Insured** less than **$5,000**, **Then** an error message must be displayed:  **“The minimum Major Trauma Benefit sum insured is $5,000.”** |
| AC23 | **Given** AC04 is active, **When** Sum insured of standalone trauma is less than $25,000**And **I enter a **Sum Insured (SI)** for **Major Trauma** that exceeds **3 times** the **Sum Insured of standalone Trauma**, **Then** the system must: - Display the following **error message**: **“The maximum Sum Insured for Major Trauma Benefit based on the Trauma Cover Sum Insured of $XXXX is $YYYY.”**  **Note:** > - **XXXX** = *Sum Insured of standalone Trauma* > - **YYYY** = *XXXX × 3* |
| AC24 | **Given** I am in the *Trauma cover* section of the *New Business Quoting Tool*, **When** I click the **"?" icon** next to any label, **Then** the system must: - Display the **corresponding tooltip** with relevant information for that label. **Label****Tooltip**Sum InsuredThe large sum insured discount bands for Trauma Recovery Cover are: - $100,000 - $249,999 - $250,000 - $499,999 - $500k + Early trauma benefitThe Early trauma benefit provides a partial payment for an additional 20 conditions which are less severe in nature. It pays the greater of $10,000 or 20% of the Trauma Recovery sum insured, up to a maximum of $100,000.Continuous trauma benefitThe Continuous trauma benefit provides continuous cover, even after a Trauma claim is made. Automatically reinstating the sum insured immediately after claim and allowing for up to 3 full trauma claims to be made under the policy for the same insured person. Major TraumaThis benefit provides additional cover on top of any Trauma Recovery Cover (TRC) for more severe conditions likely to require more financial support. Please note: A maximum of 300% of the TRC sum insured applies if TRC is less than $25,000. |

| Continuous trauma benefit | The Continuous trauma benefit provides continuous cover, even after a Trauma claim is made. Automatically reinstating the sum insured immediately after claim and allowing for up to 3 full trauma claims to be made under the policy for the same insured person.  |
| Major Trauma | This benefit provides additional cover on top of any Trauma Recovery Cover (TRC) for more severe conditions likely to require more financial support. Please note: A maximum of 300% of the TRC sum insured applies if TRC is less than $25,000. |

| AC26 | **Given **I have selected **Trauma Cover**,**When **age next birthday is more than 60**And **I select TPD on trauma cover as well**Then **an error message “The maximum Age Next Birthday for TPD on Trauma is 60” should be displayed |
