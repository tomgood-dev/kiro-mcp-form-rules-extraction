|  AC03 | **Given** I am in the *Disability Cover* section of the *New Business Quoting Tool*, **When** I select **Mortgage & Living** cover, **Then** the system must: 1. **Monthly Benefit:**     - upon placing the cursor, this should be automatically calculated and populated using agreed value or agreed value plus business rules specified      - Allow entry/update of the **Monthly Benefit** amount. 2. **Cover Type & Premium Structure:**     - Provide a dropdown to select either:         - **Annual Income(default)**         - **Monthly Mortgage **     - Provide a dropdown to select either:         - **Agreed Value**         - **Agreed Value Plus(default)**     - Provide dropdown to select **Premium Structure**          - **Stepped(default)**         - **Level to Expiry** 3. **Waiting & Benefit Periods:**     - Allow selection of the **Benefit Period** from a dropdown.         - 2 Years         - 5 Years         - To Age 65(default)         - To Age 70     - Allow selection of the **Waiting Period** from a dropdown.         - 14 Days         - 30 Days(default)         - 60 Days         - 90 Days         - 180 Days         - 365 Days         - 730 Days 4. **Optional Benefits:**     - Allow selection of the following options via checkboxes:         - **Increasing Claim (Default ticked)**         - **Income Top-up Package**         - **Specific Injury Support Benefit**         - **Immediate Assist Package**         - **Ten-Hour Benefit**         - **Mental Health Discount (should be greyed out if benefit period chosen is 2 years)** 5. **Additional Cover:**     - Display the **Split Benefit** option. |

| Business Rules | - Mortgage and Living cover for loss of earnings can be based on your annual income or monthly mortgage. If loss of earnings is based on the annual income the loss of earnings calculation based on the product selected. - AS-IS AsteronConnect stores some information and passes some information to the pricing engine. e.g. tax rates - If loss of earnings is based on Monthly Mortgage, then the annual income is disregarded, and the pricing engine calculates the monthly mortgage amount the client needs to be insured for. - There is no maximum limit for agreed value annual income basis **Agreed Value Plus : Monthly benefit calculation per life** Step1:Annual income basis: 45% of annual income up to a maximum of $7,500 per monthwhich is Annual Income \* 0.45/12Mortgage Repayment basis:115% of mortgage repayments up to a maximum of $7,500 per month  Split Monthly Benefit: Monthly Benefit calculated in step1 - Existing Net MLC Agreed Value Plus Monthly Benefit**Agreed Value: Monthly benefit calculation per life** Step1:Annual income basis:   ### 75% of After-Tax Income *** ***(Based on Income Tax Thresholds of $14,000, $48,000 and $70,000) (Annual Income up to $15600)B1= Annual Income \* 0.75 \* 0.895 (If Annual Income \> $15600 & up to $53500) B2= ((Annual Income \* 0.75) – $15600) \* 0.825 +B1 (If Annual Income \> $63500 & up to $78100) B3= ((Annual Income \* 0.75) - $63500) \* 0.70 + B2 (If Annual Income \> $78100 & up to $180,000) B4= ((Annual Income \* 0.75) - $78100) \* 0.67 + B3(If Annual Income \> $180,000) B4= ((Annual Income \* 0.75) - $18000) \* 0.61 + B4Monthly Benefit = Value calculated above/12Mortgage Repayment basis:115% of mortgage repayments   Split Monthly Benefit: Monthly Benefit calculated in step1 - Existing Net MLC Agreed Value Monthly BenefitIf there are more than one MLC and/or IP covers, maximum monthly benefit amount calculation is available in the day 2 excel sheets. Note: Current Asteron connect uses old tax rates. However, new tax rates listed below should be used in the target state. For testing purposes, to match with current Asteron connect values use old tax rates and match with below excel sheet. |
