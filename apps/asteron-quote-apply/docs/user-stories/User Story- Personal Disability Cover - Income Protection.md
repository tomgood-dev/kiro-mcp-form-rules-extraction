| Pre-Conditions |  |
| PC01 | Advisor has been on-boarded (training completed, credentials provided to advisor) |

| Acceptance Criteria |  |
|  AC01 | **Given** I am anAdviser or an Adviser staff **When** I am “creating a new quote for a personal policy” **Then** I should be provided the ability to apply for ‘Disability cover’ in the quote |
|  AC02 | **Given **I am in the Disability Cover section**When **I am creating a new business quote/application**Then **I am able to see below Disability covers - Mortgage & Living - Income Protection - Workability And I must be able to select 1 or more different covers |
|  AC03 | **Given** I am in the *Disability Cover* section of the *New Business Quoting Tool*, **When** I select **Income Protection** cover, **Then** the system must: 1. **Monthly Benefit:**     - Automatically populate the **Monthly Benefit** amount upon cursor placed in the monthly benefit field. Check business rules for calculation.      - Allow me to **update** the Monthly Benefit amount manually if needed. 2. **Cover Type & Premium Structure:**     - Provide a dropdown to select either:         - **Loss of Earning**         - **Loss of Earnings Plus (default)**     - Provide a dropdown to select **Premium Structure**          - Stepped(default)         - Level to Expiry     - Allow me to **select** the premium structure from the dropdown. 3. **Waiting & Benefit Periods:**     - Allow selection of the **Benefit Period** from a dropdown.         - 2 Years         - 5 Years         - To Age 65(default)         - To Age 70     - Allow selection of the **Waiting Period** from a dropdown.         - 14 Days         - 30 Days(default)         - 60 Days         - 90 Days         - 180 Days         - 365 Days         - 730 Days 4. **Optional Benefits:**     - Allow selection of the following options via checkboxes:         - **Increasing Claim (default ticked)**         - **Income Top-up Package**         - **Specific Injury Support Benefit**         - **Immediate Assist Package**         - **Mental Health Discount (should be greyed out if benefit period chosen is 2 years)** 5. **Additional Option:**     - Display the **Split Waiting Period** option. |
|                                AC04 | Given AC03When I select Split Waiting PeriodThen when I place cursor auto calculate and populate split monthly benefit amount using the business rules specified. And I must be provided the ability to update the automatically populated Split Monthly Benefit amount And Allow selection of the **Waiting Period** from a dropdown. - 14 Days(default) - 30 Days - 60 Days - 90 Days - 180 Days - 365 Days - 730 Days |
| AC06 | **Given** AC02 , **When** I select or click on **Income Protection**, **Then** the system must: - **Disable** the ability to add another Income Protection cover. - Ensure the **"+ Income Protection"** button is **grayed out** and **non-clickable**. |
| AC07 | **Given** AC03 is active **When** the combined sum of **Monthly Benefit** and **Split Monthly Benefit(across all policies per life)** exceeds the **Calculated Monthly Benefit** **Then** the system must: - Display the following error message: **“The maximum remaining monthly benefit for Income Protection benefit is $XXXX.”** **Where:** - **XXXX** = *Calculated Monthly Benefit − Split Monthly Benefit* (use **0** if the result is negative) |
|  AC08 | **When **I have selected the cover type**Then **I must be provided the option to ‘add’ or ‘remove’ the cover type or update inputs**And **I must be able to view the changes to the premiums on the “Progress Panel for application progress” |
| AC09 | **Given** I am in the process of creating a new business quote/application**When **I am selecting one of the following disability cover types: - Income Protection **Then **I should be able to add ‘loss of earnings’**And **the maximum monthly benefit must be calculated and displayed using the business rules specified.\*Monthly benefit is what a client can insure themselves for, the maximum amount is calculated. |
|  AC10 | **Given **I have selected ‘loss of earnings’**And **the ‘maximum monthly benefit’ amount has been calculated**Then **I must have the option to ‘split my waiting period’**And **the ‘split monthly benefit’ must calculated and displayed using the business rules specified. |
|  AC11 | **Given **I have selected ‘loss of earnings’**And **the ‘maximum monthly benefit’ has been calculated and displayed using the business rules specified**Then **I must be able to amend the ‘maximum monthly benefit’ amount. |
|  AC12 | **Given **I have selected ‘loss of earnings’**And **I have selected ‘split my waiting period’**And **the ‘split monthly benefit’ has been calculated using business rules specified**Then **I must be able to amend both the ‘maximum monthly benefit amount’ and the ‘split monthly benefit’ amount. |
| AC13 | **Given** I am in the process of creating a new business quote/application**When **I am selecting one of the following disability cover types: - Income Protection **Then **I should be able to add ‘loss of earnings plus’**And **the maximum monthly benefit must be calculated and displayed using business rules specified.\*Monthly benefit is what a client can insure themselves for, the maximum amount is calculated. |
|  AC14 | **Given **I have selected ‘loss of earnings plus’**And **the ‘maximum monthly benefit’ amount has been calculated using business rules specified**Then **I must have the option to ‘split my waiting period’**And **the ‘split monthly benefit’ must calculated and displayed. |
|  AC15 | **Given **I have selected ‘loss of earnings plus’**And **the ‘maximum monthly benefit’ has been calculated and displayed using business rules specified**Then **I must be able to amend the ‘maximum monthly benefit’ amount. |
|  AC16 | **Given **I have selected ‘loss of earnings plus’**And **I have selected ‘split my waiting period’**And **the ‘split monthly benefit’ has been calculated**Then **I must be able to amend both the ‘maximum monthly benefit amount’ and the ‘split monthly benefit’ amount. |
| AC17 | **Given **I have selected Income Protection **When **age next birthday is more than 61**Then **an error message “The maximum age next birthday for Income Protection is 61” should be displayed |
| AC18 | **Given** I am in the *Disability Cover* section of the *New Business Quoting Tool*, **When** I click the **"?" icon** next to any label, **Then** the system must: - Display the **corresponding tooltip** with relevant information for that label. **Label****Tooltip**Split Waiting PeriodSplits the total monthly benefit into two sums insured each with a different waiting period.Income Top-up PackageIncludes two benefits: - Income booster - Pays an extra 33% of the monthly benefit for the first 3 months on a full claim. - 25% income bonus - Pays an extra 25% of income earned when back at work part time but still on claim. Specific Injury Support BenefitThe Specific injury support benefit – Monthly pays a multiple of the monthly benefit for specified injuries suffered as a result of an accident. While receiving payments under this benefit no additional Living or Homemaker Support benefit is payable.Immediate Assist PackageIncludes two benefits: - Bed-Confinement - Pays a benefit for each day the insured is confined to bed during the waiting period. - Crisis Benefit - Pays a benefit upon diagnosis with one of the 11 specified conditions (including cancer, heart attack and stroke), regardless of the waiting period. |

| Business Rules | - Loss of earnings is only applicable for disability products. - Loss of earnings invokes the pricing engine. - AS-IS AsteronConnect stores some information and passes some information to the pricing engine. e.g. tax rates Income Protection: Loss of Earnings / Loss of Earnings Plus - Monthly Benefit calculated as follows if there is no MLC coverStep1:Get net income from the annual income entered in the quote screen using below (Annual Income up to $320,000)= Annual Income \* 0.75 (If Annual Income \> $320,000 & up to $560,000) = Annual Income – $320k) \* 0.50 + $240k (If Annual Income \> $560,000) = (Annual Income – $560k) \* 0.20 + $360kMaximum Monthly benefit = maximum of (Net income/12) or 30000Split Monthly benefit:Maximum Monthly benefit calculated in step 1 - minus Existing net income protection monthly benefit including split benefitIf there are more than one MLC and/or IP covers, maximum monthly benefit amount calculation is available in the day 2 excel sheets. Note: Current Asteron connect uses old tax rates. However, new tax rates listed below should be used in the target state. For testing purposes, to match with current Asteron connect values use old tax rates and match with below excel sheet. |
