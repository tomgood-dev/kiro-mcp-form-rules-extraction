|  AC25 | **Given** I am in the *Lump Sum Cover* section of the *New Business Quoting Tool*, **When** I select **Life** and sub cover **Acc TPD **as cover types, **Then** the system must: 1. **Life Cover:**     - Allow me to enter the **Sum Insured** amount for Life cover.     - Automatically default the **Premium Structure** dropdown to **Stepped**.     - Allow me to select the **Business Security** option. 2. **Acc TPD Cover:**     - Allow me to enter the **Sum Insured** amount for Acc TPD.     - Display a **Definition** dropdown with the following options:         - *Modified*         - *Any*         - *Own (default)*     - Ensure the **Premium Structure**:         - Matches the Life cover premium structure.         - Is **non-editable** if Life cover is set to **Stepped**.         - Allows selection of **Level to 70** or **Level to 80** only if Life cover is not Stepped.     - Allow me to select the **Definition** from the prepopulated list. |
|  AC26 | **Given** I am in the *Lump Sum Cover* section of the *New Business Quoting Tool*, **When** I select **Life** and sub cover **Acc Trauma** as cover types, **Then** the system must: 1. **Life Cover:**     - Allow entry of the **Sum Insured** amount.     - Pre-populate the **Premium Structure** based on the selected cover type.     - Allow me to select the **Business Security** option. 2. **Acc Trauma Cover:**     - Allow entry of the **Sum Insured** amount.     - Ensure the **Premium Structure**:         - Matches the Life cover premium structure.         - Is **non-editable** if Life cover is set to **Stepped**.         - Allows selection of **Level to 65** or **Level to 70** only if Life cover is not Stepped. 3. **Benefit Options under Acc Trauma:**     - Present the following options as **checkboxes**:         - **Early Trauma Benefit**         - **Trauma Reinstatement**         - **Continuous Trauma Benefit**             - *Note: If Trauma Reinstatement is selected, Continuous Trauma Benefit must be disabled, and vice versa.*         - **Life Cover Buyback** |
