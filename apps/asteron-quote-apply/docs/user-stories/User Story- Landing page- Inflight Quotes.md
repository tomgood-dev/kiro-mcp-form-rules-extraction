| **Scenario: Adviser/Adviser staff are navigating to the URL link to access the online new business quoting tool via their desktop ** |  |
|  AC01 | **Given** I have logged onto adviser portal**When** I click on the quote and apply link**Then **I am directed to the New Business Quoting tool UI |
|  AC02 | **Given **I am an adviser/adviser staff **When **I am in the landing page of the New Business Quoting Tool**Then **I should be able to see inflight quotes with all the statuses from day 1 |
|  AC03 | **Given **I am an adviser/adviser staff **When **application is more than 45 days from date of last modified with the statuses Application in Progress/Application in progress with Tele Interview/Pre Application**Then **That application should expire, with the status “Expired” |
| AC04 | **Given **I am a adviser/adviser staff **When ** “Quote” is more than 45 days old from date of last modified **Then** quote should not expire but if premium rates (or potentially cover types) etc change by the time they next enter it, a validation message will pop up to tell them this. |
